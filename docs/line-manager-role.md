# Prism Capital — Line Manager

## Mission

The Line Manager is **Prism’s operator** for each deal: origination of the listing, capital raise, payment confirmation, and mediation between the Project Owner and Investors.

---

## Responsibilities

1. **Create projects** (brief upload + unit model + dual fees) and submit for CEO approval  
2. **Assign Project Owner** and **invite investors** (8-char first-signin codes)  
3. **Confirm payment proofs**; approve remnant (below-min) pledges  
4. **Mediate**: message investors and the owner; inform owner when target is reached  
5. **Forward** owner profit proposals into the CEO approval path; optionally declare profit directly  
6. Decide **drawdowns** and **investor/owner withdrawals**; mark drawdowns paid  
7. Post **activity** updates; export CapEx / packs as needed  

---

## Cannot

- Approve projects or profit declarations as CEO (unless also CEO)
- Create other Line Managers (CEO only)
- View private data of other managers’ deals outside RLS
